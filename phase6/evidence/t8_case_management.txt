===================================================
Why this file exists
  The case artefacts live under phase6/cases/ (they are case management, not
  telemetry). This file is the index + audit trail that ties them back to the
  detection and correlation records in THIS evidence directory.

CASE COUNT AND THE DECISION BEHIND IT
  Cases created                          : 1
  Case ID                                : SOC002-CASE-0001
  Cases NOT created (deliberate)         : 38
  Why: the 38 alerted events in the Phase 5 window are the lab's own WinRM
       administration. Phase 6 auto-classifies them as baseline transport with a
       recorded per-event reason, so no human triage is required for them. Opening
       38 cases for routine administrative calls would recreate exactly the triage
       load Phase 6 exists to remove. They are instead recorded as a single
       "bulk_baseline_observation" inside SOC002-CASE-0001, with the per-event
       reasons left in t6_phase6_det001_v2_live_dc1.txt.
  Third candidate in the live run: 2 events (Sysmon RecId 2975 and 3045) were
       re-opened by the ESC-03 payload screen. They are NOT closed as benign:
       they are flagged as follow-up items in SOC002-CASE-0001 section 12
       (recommended follow-up) so the shape stays visible rather than being
       quietly absorbed. They are not elevated to their own case because their
       population is known lab Sysmon-configuration work.
