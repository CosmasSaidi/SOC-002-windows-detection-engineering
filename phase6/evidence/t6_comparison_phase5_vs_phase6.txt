DID THE FALSE POSITIVES GO DOWN?  Measured answer, stated precisely:
  - The DETECTION HIT COUNT did not go down: 39 encoded-PowerShell events are still detected in both phases.
  - The TRIAGE COUNT went from 39 alerts requiring manual review to 1 retained candidate(s), because 38 events were auto-classified as baseline WinRS transport.
  - The Phase 5 analyst reached the same split by hand (38 benign WinRM, 1 controlled):
    the Phase 6 baseline reproduces that human decision automatically, with the reason recorded per event.
  - What is NOT demonstrated: that this reduces false positives in general. The 38 were
    already known-benign, and no adversary telemetry exists in this lab to test the
    other direction. Treat the baseline as a triage-efficiency change, NOT as a
    measured improvement in detection quality.
