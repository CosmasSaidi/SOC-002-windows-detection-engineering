C. INVESTIGATION REASONING FOR A DET-003 RECORD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The investigation question a DET-003 record raises is NOT "is this malicious?"
It is: "who ran a base64 reconstruction, where did it come from, and did the
reconstructed data get used to execute or retrieve anything?"
  1. Account/LogonId -> is the operator authorised for this host?
  2. Parent chain (DET-002) -> was this delivered through the sanctioned WinRM
     path, an interactive logon, a scheduled task, or a service?
  3. Surrounding text -> is the decode part of a legitimate decode of config,
     a certificate, a blob, or a staged file (plumbing), or does it feed an
     execution/retrieval primitive (CTX-EXEC-ADJACENT / CTX-REMOTE-RETRIEVAL)?
  4. Payload intent -> the decoded content is read by a human. DET-003 NEVER
     executes or decodes-and-runs it.
